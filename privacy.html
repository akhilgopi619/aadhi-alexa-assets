Privacy Policy — Baby Sleep Music

Effective date: 18 August 2026

Baby Sleep Music (the "Skill") is an Amazon Alexa skill that plays an instrumental lullaby on a continuous loop. This policy explains how the Skill handles information.

Information we collect

The Skill does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal information. It does not ask for your name, contact details, or location, it does not link to any account, and it does not use advertising.

How the Skill works

When you launch the Skill, it streams a pre-recorded instrumental audio file and re-queues it so the music loops until you say "Alexa, stop." No user data is transmitted to us or to any third party as part of this process, beyond the standard request Alexa sends to run the Skill.

Children

The Skill is intended for use by adults (for example, a parent playing music for a child). It is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from children.
